    Co-operative game

    How to play

    Two teams, Blue and Red, share a grid of 25 Marvel characters. Each team has 8 hidden cards in its colour. The first team to find all of its cards wins. But be careful: if you pick the black card, your team loses instantly.

    Roles

    Spy: sees the colour of every card and gives the clues.
    Guesser: sees every card as beige until it is revealed, and picks the cards.
    A team can have several of each.

    A turn

    1. The team's spy types a clue of 1 or 2 words and a number: how many cards fit it.
    2. The guessers click cards. The number is how many tries they have.
    3. A card of your colour: keep going. A beige card or the other team's: the turn ends at once.

    After a wrong card, the tries you had left (the clue number minus the right cards) are added to your next clue.
